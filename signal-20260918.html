Research & Ideas

EfficiencyBITCOS breaks the ternary floor by counting the zerosSep 14, 2026
Intel researchers measured 29 ternary LLMs and found zeros make up as much as 51.5% of weights, breaking the equiprobable assumption behind five-trit packing at 1.625 effective bits. Their BITCOS layout — a presence bitmap plus compacted signs costing 2-minus-zero-density bits per weight — packs tighter than five-trit in 26 of 29 models and reaches 1.485 bits per weight on the sparsest. With tuned unpacking for AVX-512, AVX2, and Xe2 GPUs, kernels gain up to 1.28x and end-to-end decode up to 1.18x on CPUs and 1.27x on GPUs.
arXiv 2609.16338 — Sep 14
AgentsDream-RSI replays history instead of re-running the searchSep 14, 2026
Zheng and sixteen co-authors make exploration programmable with a thin orchestration layer over an unchanged coding agent, then treat accumulated discovery trees as a replay simulator for off-policy policy evaluation. Dreaming in the replay world gives immediate cheap feedback for refining exploration strategy, and the improved policy redeploys online to grow the simulator pool further. Across algorithm engineering, mathematical optimization, and GPU kernel work, discovery quality holds or improves at substantially lower cost — self-improvement where the simulator is free because it is the past.
